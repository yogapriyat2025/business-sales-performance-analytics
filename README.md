# Business Sales Performance Analytics

An end-to-end business sales analysis project using the Sample Superstore dataset, Microsoft Excel, and Power BI. The project focuses on data quality checks, exploratory analysis, business performance metrics, and an interactive dashboard.

## Project Overview

The objective is to understand sales and profitability across product categories, regions, customer segments, years, and individual products. The workflow includes preparing the dataset in Excel, analyzing it with PivotTables and PivotCharts, and presenting key findings in Power BI.

## Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel** — data validation, cleaning, PivotTables, and PivotCharts
- **Microsoft Power BI** — data modelling, KPI cards, interactive visuals, and dashboard design
- **Sample Superstore dataset** — 9,994 order-line records

## Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | 2.30M |
| Total Profit | 286.40K |
| Total Orders | 5,009 |
| Total Customers | 793 |

## Analysis Performed

- Sales and profit by product category
- Sales and profit by region
- Sales and profit by sub-category
- Year-wise sales trend (2014–2017)
- Sales and profit by customer segment
- Top 10 products by sales
- Top 10 products by profit

## Key Business Findings

- Technology is the highest-performing category in both sales and profit.
- The West region records the highest sales, followed by the East.
- The Consumer segment contributes approximately 50.6% of total sales.
- Sales show an overall increase across the 2014–2017 period.

1. Open the Power BI `.pbix` file in Power BI Desktop to explore the dashboard.
2. Open the Excel workbook to review the data checks, PivotTables, and charts.
3. Refer to the report for the project workflow, analysis, and findings.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates a practical business analytics workflow: validating data, analyzing sales and profitability, identifying performance patterns, and communicating insights through an interactive dashboard.
